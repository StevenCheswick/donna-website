Social Security Offset Calculations

In domestic relations actions in Pennsylvania, it is recognized that a civil service employee may exclude from equitable distribution that portion of pension estimated to be received in lieu of Social Security (Cornbleth v. Cornbleth, 397 PA. Super.421, 580 A.2d 369 (1990)).

Contact us if you have a case where an employee is not paying into Social Security and you require a Hypothetical Social Security Offset for use in a Present Value calculation.
